Prime Number Reaction Time Experiment

Welcome! This experiment will measure your reaction time when judging whether numbers are prime.

Instructions

  • You will see numbers on the screen, one at a time.
  • Your task is to judge whether each number is PRIME or NOT PRIME as quickly and accurately as possible.
  • A fixation cross (+) will appear before each number.
  • You will receive brief feedback after each response.
  • The experiment consists of a practice block followed by 4 main blocks.
  • You can take breaks between blocks.
  • Try to respond as quickly as possible while maintaining accuracy.
